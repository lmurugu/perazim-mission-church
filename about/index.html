About us

The place of great breakthrough

Perazim Mission Church is a Pentecostal and Evangelical family of faith in Manyatta, Embu, on the slopes of Mount Kenya.

Our name

“Perazim” comes from Baal-Perazim, the place where King David said, “The Lord has broken through my enemies before me, like a breaking flood” (2 Samuel 5:20). It means “Lord of breakthroughs”, and it's why we call ourselves the place of great breakthrough.

Our mission

Transforming the city of Embu with the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Where we are

Our main campus is Embu Central Campus in Manyatta, Embu County. Everyone is welcome to join us on Sunday.

What we believe

Faith, fellowship and the future

The basics of our faith, in plain words. Our full statement of faith is in our church constitution.

Faith

The Bible is the inspired word of God. We believe in the Holy Trinity, and in Jesus Christ as God, born of a virgin, who died for our sins, rose bodily and ascended to heaven. We believe in salvation through faith in Jesus, in baptism and infilling with the Holy Spirit with speaking in other tongues as the initial evidence, and in divine healing. We also believe God heals through medical science.

Fellowship

The church is the body of Christ, and believers should meet together for worship and fellowship. We share Holy Communion. We baptise believers by full immersion in water, as a public witness to the new life they have received.

The future

We believe in the personal return of Jesus Christ, who will gather his church to himself, and in his reign on earth for a thousand years. We believe God will judge all people finally.

Article of Faith

  1. We believe that the Bible is the inspired, authoritative, inerrant and infallible word of God (2 Peter 1:21; 2 Timothy 3:16).
  2. We believe in the Holy Trinity: the Godhead, consisting of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. These three are one (2 Corinthians 13:14).
  3. We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, that He is God. We believe in His virgin birth, His atoning death, His bodily resurrection and His ascension into heaven (John 10:30; John 17:5; Philippians 2:6; Colossians 1:19; Titus 2:13; 1 Timothy 3:16).
  4. We believe in personal salvation through Jesus Christ, upon confession with the mouth and belief in the heart. We believe in the baptism and infilling with the Holy Ghost, with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues, and in manifesting the fruit of the Spirit (Isaiah 28:11; Luke 24:49; Acts 2:1-4; Romans 10:9-10; Ephesians 2:8; Acts 10:46; Galatians 5:16, 22).
  5. We believe in divine healing, obtained on the basis of the atonement and faith in the healer, Jesus (Matthew 9:12; Mark 2:17; Mark 6:13; James 5:14-15; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53; Psalm 103:3).
  6. We also believe that God heals through medical science, and that conventional medicine is God's way of saving lives He cares for.

Article of Fellowship

  1. We believe that the universal church and the local church are the body of Jesus Christ, the habitation of God through the Spirit, with divine appointments for the fulfilment of the Great Commission. Each believer born of the Spirit is an integral part of the General Assembly and the church of the firstborn. Individual members of the local church must come together for worship and fellowship (Ephesians 1:22-23; Hebrews 10:25).
  2. We believe in partaking of Holy Communion (Acts 2:42-45; 1 Corinthians 11:26). We believe in believers' baptism in water by immersion, as public testimony to the world of the inward work of the grace of God. Immersion and the rising from the water symbolise the Christian dying to sin and to the world, and rising with Jesus unto newness of life. We do not consider baptism before the individual's personal experience of salvation to be scriptural (Matthew 28:19; Romans 6:4).

Article of the Future

  1. We believe in the personal and pre-millennial return of Jesus Christ, to receive unto Himself the church, which is referred to as the "Rapture". We believe that Christ will reign on earth for one thousand years (the Millennium) with His saints (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Titus 2:13; Revelation 21 and 22).
  2. We believe in the existence of Satan and demons. We believe that the devil does not possess the attributes of God, and that God restrains him. We believe in casting out demons. We believe that they, with those whose names are not written in the book of life, will finally be judged by God and consigned to everlasting punishment in the lake of fire (Revelation 19:20; 20:10-15).
Our constitution, in brief

How the church is run

A short summary of our 2017 constitution.

Our purpose

To preach the gospel of Jesus Christ, hold crusades, open-air meetings and personal witness, support missions, train ministers and church workers, encourage home fellowship for Bible study and prayer, and care for the community.

Membership

Any born-again Christian may join. Members fill in a membership form, and the pastor and local church council decide. Members are encouraged to witness, live by the Bible, worship regularly, take part in church activities, and give tithes and offerings.

Leadership

The Bishop is the National Elder and leads the whole church. Each local church has a pastor, who chairs the local church council, with deacons and elders. Council members and office bearers serve three-year terms.
