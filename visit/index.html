First-visit questions

How long is the service?

The Bible hour runs from 9:00 to 10:00 AM, and the main service runs from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Where exactly is the church?

We meet at Embu Central Campus in Manyatta, Embu County. Use “Get directions” above, or call or WhatsApp us for landmarks from where you are.

Can I bring my family?

Yes. Families and children are welcome.

Will anyone know I'm coming?

Send us a WhatsApp message saying you're coming this Sunday, and our welcome team will be glad to meet you.

What language is the service in?

Swahili and English.

What should I wear?

Dress however you like, as long as it is decent and you're mindful of others.

What else should I know?

Ask us on WhatsApp about children's programmes and parking. We'd rather answer you personally than guess.
