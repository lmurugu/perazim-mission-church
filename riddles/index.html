Riddles from Scripture

Riddles from Scripture for families and youth. Tap a riddle to reveal the answer.

Try a riddleMore for the family
mediumI lived longer than any mortal man recorded in Scripture, yet I died before my father did. Who am I?

Methuselah

hardOut of the eater came something to eat; out of the strong came something sweet. What is this riddle and what is its answer?

A lion's carcass with honey inside

easyI had no earthly father, no earthly mother, and was never born as a baby; yet God fashioned me from the virgin soil of Eden. Who am I?

Adam

easyI was neither a prophet, priest, nor king, yet God opened my mouth to speak with a human voice to rebuke a wayward prophet who was blind to the Angel of the Lord. Who am I?

Balaam's Donkey

easyKing David named a battlefield after me when God broke through his enemies like a rushing torrent of bursting waters. What is the name of this place of Great Breakthrough?

Baal-Perazim

hardI was King of Salem and priest of God Most High, without genealogy, without beginning of days or end of life, foreshadowing the eternal priesthood of Jesus Christ. Who am I?

Melchizedek

easyI was short in physical stature and despised as a tax collector, but I climbed a sycamore fig tree to catch a glimpse of Jesus in Jericho. Who am I?

Zacchaeus

mediumI offered only five barley loaves and two small fish from my lunch, yet in the hands of the Lord, my meal satisfied five thousand men with twelve baskets of leftovers. Who was I?

The Boy with the Loaves and Fish
