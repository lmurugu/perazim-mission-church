Where do you go from here?

Whether you're visiting for the first time or you've been coming for years, there's a clear next step for you. Pick the one that fits.

Choose your step

I'm new here

We baptise believers by full immersion in water, as a public witness to the new life in Christ. Tell us when you're ready and we'll talk it through with you.

Ways to get involved

Our church is built on people who meet in homes, reach out to others and care for their neighbours. These are the core ways we serve.

Fellowships

Gents, ladies and youth fellowships meet on the fourth Sunday of the month, after the main service. Fellowship leaders meet on Wednesdays at 7:00 PM.

Home fellowship

Small gatherings in homes for Bible study and prayer.

Outreach and evangelism

Open-air meetings, crusades and personal witness, sharing the good news of Jesus in Embu and beyond.

Care for the community

Practical help for families and neighbours, because we want to see the city of Embu transformed.

Want to know which groups meet, and when? Ask us on WhatsApp.
