Privacy

Your privacy on this site

This website does not ask you to sign in, does not run advertising and does not track you. Here is what it does use.

Contact us

What this website does not do

No accounts or forms

You don’t sign in, and no page on this site collects your name, phone number or email address.

No tracking or ads

The site has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

One saved setting

If you choose a light, dark or automatic colour theme, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. We never receive it.

Other services the site uses

Some content comes from other companies. When your browser loads it, that company can see that your device visited, as with any website.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

Fonts

The text styles load from Google Fonts.

Video and maps

The latest message on the home page is a YouTube video, sermon pictures come from YouTube, and the map on the Visit page is Google Maps.

Links to other apps

Buttons for WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, email and phone calls open those apps. What you send there is shared with the church and with that service.
